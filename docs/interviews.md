# Interviews

## Interview 1 — Production Group (Liberty Theatre)
- Booking method: WhatsApp messages and direct phone calls
- Required details: school name, number of students, preferred date, location
- Approver: Production manager
- Common problems: double-booking, late confirmations, missing payment proof
- Preferred payment method: Upload proof for now; mobile integration later
- Extra feature request: Calendar view to avoid clashes

## Interview 2 — Client (High School Teacher)
- Booking method: Usually phone call or WhatsApp
- Required details: show type, date, number of students
- Approver: Head of drama club + school deputy
- Common problems: delays in communication; unclear pricing breakdown
- Preferred payment method: Mpesa but screenshot also okay
- Extra feature request: Ability to see total cost before calling
