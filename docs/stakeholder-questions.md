# Stakeholder interview questions (use these to talk to 2-3 productions / clients)

1. How do you currently accept bookings? (phone/WhatsApp/physical)
2. What information is required to confirm a booking? (venue, audience size, date, payment)
3. Who approves bookings in your team?
4. What is the most common booking problem you experience?
5. What fields should users complete when registering for your production?
6. Do you require proof of payment? If yes, which formats? (image/pdf)
7. What performance categories do you use?
8. Would you prefer manual payment proof or direct mobile payment (Mpesa) integration later?
